Sidetrack 0.4.2 adds Plex as a fourth service and widens Navidrome into the whole Subsonic family. Playlists can now be renamed and deleted. Playing a track from an album no longer throws away your queue, and a replaced queue can be restored for 8 seconds. It updates in place, so nothing needs reinstalling.

## Queue

- Album, playlist and radio rows have a new **Play** action. It plays that track now and returns to your queue afterwards. Shuffle, Repeat context and the pre-shuffle order are kept. If the track was already further down the queue, it is moved rather than played twice.
- Tapping a row still plays from that row onwards. When that replaces your queue, `Queue replaced | Undo` appears for 8 seconds. Undo restores the old queue and resumes the interrupted track where you left off. Any other play, dismissing the bar or waiting withdraws the offer.
- Starting an album whose next page is still loading now keeps adding pages to the queue as they arrive.

## Playlists

- Rename and delete are checked per playlist and appear only where the service allows them. A collaborator may add tracks without owning the list.
- The new playlist form starts from your recent tracks.

## Search

- Search opens on its own page with your recent terms underneath. Leaving and coming back keeps the query and the scope.
- The last 20 searches are kept, newest first. A repeated search moves to the top. Terms shorter than two letters are not stored, and you can remove single terms.

## Plex

- Sign-in is a PIN on plex.tv, so Sidetrack never sees your Plex password. The token is stored encrypted on the phone.
- Plex supports music libraries, playlists, artists, albums, search, playback progress and scrobbling. Likes use Plex's own rating system. Rename and delete work on regular playlists.
- Plex has no song radio, loudness normalisation, gapless playback or quality selection. Playlist creation is missing too, because Plex needs a first track to create one. These controls stay where they are, dimmed and inert, instead of failing.

## Subsonic family

- Navidrome, Nextcloud Music and Subsonic are now three entries in the service picker, listed alphabetically. They share one adapter. Each entry keeps its own credentials, queue, history and resume point, so two servers keep two libraries.
- Existing Navidrome installs upgrade in place and stay signed in.

## Playback

- A track the service refuses is now skipped. Before, Sidetrack stayed on it, took the silence for a lost connection and reloaded it three times. Now the queue moves on as after a finished track, autoplay included, and never with Repeat track. A notice appears only if the queue really moved.
- A finished track is no longer reloaded, and the player is no longer rebuilt between tracks.
- A rolling playback log records what the player did, so stalls and skips can be diagnosed.
- The sleep timer reads `Pauses in 23 min`, `Pauses after this track` and `Paused by sleep timer`. Home shows `TIMER DONE` when it has finished. The old wording was cut off there.
- Episode notes can be read from the keypad, and podcasts have their own Now Playing card.

## Upgrading

Package and signature are unchanged, so the app updates in place and keeps its history, queue, resume point, settings and search history.

## Known issues

- Plex uses only the first music library on the server and needs a claimed server.
- Jellyfin servers older than 12.0 are refused at sign-in.

## Under the hood

- One Media3 service now runs playback for the Subsonic family, Jellyfin and Plex.
- A release can no longer ship without the native Spotify bridge. The About screen states which Spotify engine your APK carries.
- Built with Sidekit 1.11.0.
